### 1b. Terrain 3D: what it is, resolution, heights
- Terrain 3D is meant as a ground for ArcGIS 3D apps | "The Terrain 3D layer provides global elevation surface to use in ArcGIS 3D applications." | https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/7029fb60158543ad845c7e1527af11e4?f=json | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
- Its licence is the Esri Master License Agreement; not for exporting tiles | "This work is licensed under the Esri Master License Agreement." / "This layer is not intended to be used to export tiles for offline." | same item JSON | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
- Heights are orthometric (sea level), built from many national sources, 1000 m to 50 cm | "Heights are orthometric (in meters) and are based on multiple sources." / "ranging from 1000m - 50cm" | https://elevation3d.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/WorldElevation3D/Terrain3D/ImageServer?f=json | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
- The tile cache is LERC, 256 x 256, Web Mercator, levels 0-16; level 16 = 2.389 m per pixel in Web Mercator | `"format":"LERC"`, last LOD `{'level': 16, 'resolution': 2.388657133974685}` | same service JSON | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
- **inference**: level 16 is about 1.53 m on the ground at Żabi Koń (50.16° N) and 1.64 m at Lauterbrunnen (46.60° N): Esri's 3D ground is coarser than our 1 m (0.5 m CH) DEM even where it was built from the same lidar.
- Level-16 tiles exist at Żabi Koń and on Malta (tilemap `"data":[1]` for both) | `{"data":[1],...,"valid":true}` | https://elevation3d.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/WorldElevation3D/Terrain3D/ImageServer/tilemap/16/22181/36364/1/1?f=json | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
- World Elevation sources at our places (identify on Esri's DataExtents map service; first row = finest source):
  - Żabi Koń and Tatras (PL): GUGiK 1 m, 2018-2021, datum EVRF2007-PL | `'ProductName': 'Poland_DTM1m', 'PixelSize (meters)': '1', 'VerticalDatum': 'EVRF2007-PL'` | https://elevation.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/WorldElevation/DataExtents/MapServer/identify (point 19.7563, 50.1560) | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
  - Lauterbrunnen (CH): swissALTI3D 0.5 m, 2019-2025, LN02 | `'Switzerland_swissALTI3D_50cm', 'PixelSize (meters)': '0.5', 'VerticalDatum': 'LN02'` | same, point 7.907, 46.596 | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
  - Súľov (SK): ÚGKK SR 1 m, 2017-2023, Baltic 1957 | `'Slovakia_DTM1m', 'PixelSize (meters)': '1', 'VerticalDatum': 'Baltic_1957'` | same, point 18.575, 49.17 | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
  - Montserrat (ES): IGN 2 m, 2015-2020, Alicante | `'Spain_DTM2m', 'PixelSize (meters)': '2', 'VerticalDatum': 'Alicante'` | same, point 1.81, 41.593 | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
  - Dingli (MT): no national lidar; Airbus WorldDEM4Ortho 24 m, EGM2008 | `'Airbus_WorldDEM4Ortho_24m', 'PixelSize (meters)': '24.73764', 'VerticalDatum': 'EGM2008'` | same, point 14.383, 35.8565 | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
- The tiled 3D services also hold Vantor Precision3D in parts of the globe (not in the point Elevation service) | "The tiled services (Terrain 3D, ...) also include an additional data source from Vantor's Precision3D covering parts of the globe." | https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/3af669838f594b378f90c10f98e46a7f?f=json | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
- In a SceneView, z values with `absolute-height` are heights above sea level, the same kind of height as Terrain 3D | "Elements are placed at an absolute elevation (z-value) above sea level." | https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@arcgis/core@5.1.27/symbols/support/ElevationInfo.d.ts | accessed 2026-11-01 | VERIFIED
- **inference**: in an ArcGIS SceneView over Terrain 3D, our anchor heights e1/e2 (PL EVRF2007-PL, CH LN02, SK Baltic 1957, ES Alicante) can be given as `absolute-height` z without any geoid grid: Esri's ground in these countries is built from the same national DEM in the same datum, so the line meets the ground to within the DEM's resampling (~1.5 m pixels). Malta (Esri: EGM2008 24 m) and the countries where Esri's source differs from ours need checking. In CesiumJS, `ArcGISTiledElevationTerrainProvider` passes the orthometric heights to a globe that expects ellipsoidal ones; the whole scene is then ~30-50 m low but self-consistent, as long as nothing ellipsoidal (Google tiles, Cesium World Terrain) is mixed in (R3 to confirm).

### 1g. Esri verdict (inference, for the coordinator)
- Terrain: Terrain 3D is free of metering (not on the price list; the SDK sends no key), anonymous, CORS-enabled, documented by Esri for CesiumJS. Resolution: level 16 ≈ 1.5-1.6 m on the ground, built from the national 1 m / 0.5 m lidar in PL/CH/SK (2 m ES, 24 m Malta). - Gate status: passes the budget (hard stop), accounts (only Location Platform) and non-commercial/donations gates; fidelity is "our orthophoto quality on a ~1.5 m ground", not a photorealistic mesh.
