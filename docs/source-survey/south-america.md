# Elevation sources: South America (survey of 2026-10-31)

Notes of the survey in [../source-survey.md](../source-survey.md), one section per group of countries as researched; the rows are in [sources.json](sources.json).

## g12: southern South America: Brazil (and its states), Argentina, Chile, Uruguay, Paraguay, Falklands (2026-10-31)

Scripts: `scripts/g12-sa-south/` (`ls.py` directory listings, `s3ls.py` S3 listings, `cog.py` /vsicurl/ windows, `wcs.py` DescribeCoverage + GetCoverage, `pdftext.py`, `make_json.py`), samples: `dl/g12-sa-south/`. Rows: [sources.json](sources.json) (15).

Summary: no country of the group has an open national LiDAR DTM. The usable sources are two photogrammetric (image-matching) state/national models published as plain files: **Espírito Santo** (2 m DTM + DSM, whole state, public S3, whole-metre values) and **Uruguay** (2.5 m DTM, whole country, CC-BY-like national licence, little cliff terrain). The famous highline places (Rio's domes, Patagonia, Cajón del Maipo) have nothing better than FABDEM in open form today. Watch list: Rio Grande do Sul's LiDAR (2025–2026, Aparados da Serra canyons), São Paulo state's LiDAR (2023–2024), Rio's 2019/2024 LiDAR (city), Argentina's IGN 5 m sectors (site down today). For all of South America, **ANADEM** (a 30 m DTM for South America, on OpenTopography) is an alternative bare-earth gap filler to FABDEM; its licence was not checked here.

### Brazil (IBGE, national): 20 m photogrammetric sheets only
- **Data**: IBGE's "Modelo Digital de Elevação 1:25.000" for the RJ25 project (Rio de Janeiro state, 315 sheet ZIPs), SC25 (Santa Catarina) and SP-MG-GO 1:50 000. Metadata (RJ25): automatic image correlation (SOCET SET ATE) of 1:30 000 photos, **20 × 20 m grid**, "± 5 metros na componente altimétrica"; it is a surface model in effect ("pode conter elevação de elementos com altura significativa"). Some RJ25 sheets are classified ("folhas … sigilosas"). No national DTM finer than this; the Army's BDGEx holds 1:25 000/1:50 000 products.
- **Access**: static Apache directory https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/modelos_digitais_de_superficie/modelo_digital_de_elevacao_mde/ (`rj25/tif/mde_<sheet>_v1.zip`, index `indice_MDE_RJ25_v2.shp.zip`); ranges supported.
- **Licence**: no licence file in the folder; IBGE products are generally free with citation (not verified for these).
- **Quick tests**: listing (rj25/tif 315 entries), HEAD of `mde_2685a3no_v1.zip` (12 985 bytes, Apache 2.4), metadata PDF read.
- **Fit**: none: 20 m photogrammetry is no gain over FABDEM/GLO-30. Priority none.
- **Links**: the folder above; `rj25/informacoes_tecnicas/Metadados_MDE_RJ25.pdf`.

### Brazil – Espírito Santo (IEMA / GEOBASES): 2 m DTM and DSM as tiled .img files on public S3
- **Data**: MAPES 2012–2015 (contract IEMA 001/2012, HIPARC): an aerial photogrammetric survey of the whole state (~46 000 km², UltraCam Lp, GSD 25 cm pan / 55 cm RGBI, 32 509 photos), with an MDE (surface model) and an MDT (terrain model), 0.25 m orthomosaic, hydrography and contours at 1:10 000/1:25 000. Both models **2 m**, in ~550 tiles of 10 × 10 km. Heights orthometric (control points converted with MAPGEO2010, Imbituba datum). No newer elevation survey (2019–2020 is a KOMPSAT satellite orthomosaic).
- **Access**: a public Ceph/S3 bucket of the state, https://pr-geobases-public.one.s3.es.gov.br/ (ListObjectsV2 works without a key; also as https://one.s3.es.gov.br/pr-geobases-public/…): `MAP_ES_2012_2015/MDT/<col>_<row>.img` (+ `.rrd` overviews, `.igw`, `.xml`), `MAP_ES_2012_2015/MDE/<col>_<row>.img`, URL lists `MAP_ES_2012_2015_MDT_URL_LIST.txt` and `_MDE_URL_LIST.txt`, the tile grid `MAP_ES_2012_2015_ARTICULACAO_10X10_KM.zip`. MDT 37.6 GB, MDE 31.1 GB, orthophoto ECWs 223 GB. Linked from https://geobases.es.gov.br/downloads.
- **Format**: Erdas Imagine `.img`, **uint16 (whole metres)**, 5001 × 5001 px, tiled 64 × 64, uncompressed (51 MB per tile), external `.rrd` overviews: GDAL reads windows over HTTP, no download of whole tiles needed. EPSG:31984 (SIRGAS 2000 / UTM 24S). Pixel edges at …999.0 / …001.0, i.e. **centres on even metres** (`grid_origin` 0.5) and tiles overlapping by one pixel. MDT nodata 0; the MDE declared no nodata.
- **Versions**: one survey.
- **Licence**: none found: the bucket is public and the portal offers the files for download, but no terms are stated (a ZIP "Convenios e Normatizacao GEOBASES" of 33 MB was not opened). Ask GEOBASES (faleconosco@geobases.es.gov.br) before use on the public site.
- **Orthophoto**: GeoServer WMS https://ide.geobases.es.gov.br/geoserver/ows, layer `geonode:iema_ortofotomosaico_es_025m_2012-2015` (0.25 m), also `geonode:ijsn-ortofotomosaico-es-kompsat-3-3a-2019-2020`; GetMap 1.3.0 in EPSG:4326 at Pedra Azul: 512 px JPEG 47 kB in 1.4 s, clear granite slabs, no watermark. (The WMS also lists 5 m derived MDT layers, north/south.)
- **Quick tests (2026-10-31)**: S3 listing (4497 objects under MAP_ES_2012_2015); `/vsicurl/` MDT `37_782.img`: EPSG:31984, 2 m, blocks 64 × 64, uint16, open 4.3 s, 200 × 200 window 2.0 s, 100% whole metres; MDE `20_771.img` (808–905 m) and MDT `27_769.img` the same layout.
- **Fit**: a new small subclass: a fixed 10 km grid (`<col>_<row>` from the articulation) of tiled files read in windows, as `DegreeBlocks` does for 1° files; `grid_origin` 0.5; drop the duplicated edge pixel. Special: whole-metre heights (slopes and clearances coarse, as Malta 2018), image-matching models (cliff edges smoothed, vegetation partly in the "DTM"), surveys 2012–2015. Effort M.
- **Highline**: Pedra Azul, Forno Grande, Pancas, Itaguaçu: Brazil's main granite-dome highline area with Rio. Priority **medium** (licence unclear, 2 m whole metres, photogrammetric).
- **Open questions**: the licence; how well a 2 m whole-metre photogrammetric DTM shows dome edges (compare with GLO-30 and a known line at Pedra Azul).

### Brazil – Rio de Janeiro (city, IPP): LiDAR 2019 only as a rendered 5 m map
- **Data**: the city flew LiDAR in 2019 and 2024 (8 pts/m², Topocart; papers by IPP/UERJ). Published: "Modelo Digital de Terreno derivado de levantamento Lidar de 2019 reamostrado para 5 metros", a MapServer (rendered hypsometry, EPSG:31983); `identify` returns pixel values (373.67 m at Pão de Açúcar), so values exist on the server, but there is no export. No DSM, no point clouds in the city's ArcGIS Hub (searched for LiDAR, MDT, nuvem de pontos).
- **Licence**: IPP items: "uso público, citadas as fontes e sem alteração das informações originais".
- **Orthophoto**: ImageServers `Imagens/Mosaico_1999 … Mosaico_2025` (2025: 0.57 m, 3-band U8); `exportImage` in EPSG:3857 at Pão de Açúcar 512 px in 2.9 s, fine.
- **Fit**: none until the 1 m LiDAR products are published (ask IPP: the domes of Rio are one of the world's best-known highline areas). Rio de Janeiro **state**: only IBGE's RJ25 20 m (row br); no state LiDAR found. Priority low.

### Brazil – Santa Catarina (SDS, SIGSC): 1 m DTM/DSM of 2010–2012, portal unreachable
- **Data** (from the SIGSC notes found by search): aerial survey 2010–2012, orthophotos 0.39 m RGB/IR, **MDT and MDS 1.0 m GeoTIFF**, EPSG:31982; downloads per selected area, one file at a time. A UDESC study reports systematic errors in the state's MDT.
- **Quick tests**: sigsc.sc.gov.br and sigsc2.ciasc.sc.gov.br (200.19.215.115) time out on port 443 from Poland and refuse connections from the fetch tool: down or geo-blocked; no test.
- **Fit/priority**: unclear until reachable; photogrammetric 1 m; low for now. Highline: Serra do Rio do Rastro, Urubici, the rim of Aparados da Serra.

### Brazil – São Paulo: city LiDAR point clouds; state LiDAR flown, not yet found published
- **City**: LiDAR 2017 (~10 pts/m²) and 2020, LAZ on GeoSampa and AWS Open Data (`s3://laz-m3dc-pmsp`, `s3://ept-m3dc-pmsp`, sa-east-1; also OpenTopography BR17_SaoPaulo). Point clouds only (ground class = "MDT"). Little highline terrain (Pico do Jaraguá).
- **State**: SEMIL's MDE 30 m from 1:50 000 contours (one 1.28 GB RAR); a state-wide aerial survey with LiDAR (4 pts/m², 10 in the metro area) and 25 cm orthophotos was planned for 2023–2024 (IGC/CDHU); publication not found. Would cover Serra da Mantiqueira (Pedra do Baú) and Serra do Mar.
- **Fit**: point clouds need a new driver (PDAL → rasters), effort L, not worth it for the city. Priority low; watch the state LiDAR.

### Brazil – Minas Gerais, Distrito Federal, Paraná (short)
- **Minas Gerais**: IDE-Sisema WCS has only TOPODATA 30 m (SRTM) and derivatives (GetCapabilities checked). Serra do Cipó has nothing better than FABDEM. None.
- **Distrito Federal**: geoportal.seduh.df.gov.br did not answer; no open DTM found (1:2000 restitution exists). Few cliffs. None.
- **Paraná**: no public state DTM found (studies use 20 m grids from contours or TOPODATA). None.

### Brazil – Rio Grande do Sul: LiDAR in progress
After the 2024 floods the federal MIDR is flying LiDAR (MDT/MDS) over the east of the state and the state (SEMA, edital 0069/2025) the west and part of the north coast. Publication terms unknown. The east includes the Aparados da Serra / Serra Geral canyons (Itaimbezinho, Fortaleza): if published openly, a high-priority source. None today.

### Argentina (IGN): 30 m national, 5 m sectors; IGN unreachable today
- **Data**: MDE-Ar v2.1, 30 m, from SRTM + ALOS, ~2 m vertical (CONAE), vertical reference EGM96 per CONAE (to verify); "MDE 5m": an aerophotogrammetric DEM with sub-metre vertical accuracy (~0.5 m) for limited sectors shown on IGN's coverage map; a review mentions 0.5 m LiDAR DEMs for some urban areas.
- **Access**: IGN's search page `ign.gob.ar/NuestrasActividades/Geodesia/ModeloDigitalElevaciones/Busqueda`. On 2026-10-31 all ign.gob.ar hosts failed DNS (Cloudflare DoH: "No Reachable Authority at delegation ign.gob.ar"), so neither the coverage map, the terms (`ign.gob.ar/descargas/tyc1.html`) nor a file could be checked.
- **Fit**: unclear (probably index + files per sheet). Priority low until the 5 m coverage of Patagonia/Mendoza and the licence are known. Highline: El Chaltén, Bariloche (Frey, Catedral), Mendoza, Córdoba: very high.
- **Open questions**: does the 5 m cover Bariloche/El Chaltén? licence (IGN terms)? format and CRS of the 5 m sheets.

### Argentina – Córdoba (IDECOR): the IGN 5 m DEM as a WCS (broken layer)
- IDECOR's GeoServer (https://idecor-ws.mapascordoba.gob.ar/geoserver/idecor/wcs) serves "Modelo Digital de Elevaciones: Aerofotogamétrico 5m" (`idecor__dem_5m_cba_ext`, "que distribuye el IGN", vertical sub-metre) over the Sierras de Córdoba (−65.07…−63.94, −32.06…−30.95), plus MDE-Ar v2.0 30 m, MERIT 90 m and 5 m slope/aspect, in EPSG:22174.
- **Quick tests**: DescribeCoverage shows a broken native envelope (Y 3.9–7.6 M m, 61 966 × 731 256 px) and GetCoverage refuses every box ("Empty intersection after subsetting") with either axis order; the derived 5 m aspect layer works only with the axes swapped (GeoServer's north-east axis order), 100 × 100 px of 5.00004 m. Nodata 32767 suggests int16 heights.
- **Fit**: `WcsTiles` if fixed, better IGN's own sheets. Los Gigantes / Condorito are medium highline terrain. Priority low.

### Chile: nothing open beyond 12.5 m ALOS PALSAR
- The national geoportal (IDE Chile, Bienes Nacionales) lists 19 elevation records: CIREN's ALOS PALSAR 12.5 m DEMs per region (an upsampled SRTM, not better than GLO-30), a 4 m DEM of Isla de Pascua from contours, and DWG surveys of sectors of Valparaíso/O'Higgins/Metropolitana. datos.gob.cl: no LiDAR. OpenTopography: only research surveys (San Ramón fault from Pléiades, Chuculay fault). LiDAR exists in agencies and cities but is not published; SAF/IGM sell their products. Cajón del Maipo and Torres del Paine: FABDEM only. None.

### Uruguay (IDE Uruguay): national 2.5 m DTM as sheets, open licence
- **Data**: the national project (LPI 1/2015, Consorcio Topocart/AT, flights 2017–2018, finished 2019-10): orthoimages 32 cm (national) and 10 cm true ortho (86 towns, ~1 230 km²); a **national MDT 2.5 m** in 12 "remesas" of ~5 × 5 km sheets (6 597 sheets in the index); for the towns an MDT 1 m and an **MDS 10 cm** (1 km sheets). Method (urban quality report): point clouds by image correlation (Match-T, Inpho) and editing on 3D stations, i.e. **photogrammetric**, heights orthometric on **EGM2008**; vertical accuracy required ≤ 0.30 m (95%) on control points; the national product presumably the same method. New: "Vuelo Minas 2025" (town of Minas: LiDAR LAS, MDS 10 cm, 3D model).
- **Access**: static Apache directory https://visualizador.ide.uy/descargas/datos/: `CN_Remesa_<nn>/01_MDT/02_MDT_GeoTIFF_Resolucion_250cm/<sheet>_MDT_Remesa_<nn>.tif` (~20 MB each; also LAS), urban `CU_Remesa_<nn>/01_MDT_MDS/<nn>_Ciudad_<XXX>/…`. Index: `Grillas/Nacional/Articulacion_Ortoimagenes_Nacional.shp` (fields `Nombre`, `Remesa`; SIRGAS-ROU98 geographic) gives the URL of every sheet. `/descargas/` itself answers 403, `/descargas/datos/` lists. F5 load balancer (cookies), range requests.
- **Format**: GeoTIFF float32, uncompressed, one row per strip (no internal tiles: a window costs about a full-width row band; files are small enough to download whole), nodata −32767, EPSG:5382 (UTM 21S) or 5383 (UTM 22S) by zone, **pixel edges not on whole metres** (origin 644100.2085, 6254652.5919), `AREA_OR_POINT=Point`; values float (0% whole cm).
- **Licence**: "Licencia de Datos Abiertos – Uruguay" v0.1 (PDF in the folder): data "para uso comercial y no comercial podrán ser: enajenados, reproducidos, distribuidos, publicados, … transformados"; credit "el nombre del proveedor", "la referencia a la 'Licencia de Datos Abiertos – Uruguay'" and the dataset; changes must be mentioned. Compatible with the public site and with own hosting.
- **Orthophoto**: GeoWebCache WMTS (REST) `https://mapas.ide.uy/geoserver-raster/gwc/service/wmts/rest/ortofotos:ortofoto_nacional/raster/EPSG:3857/EPSG:3857:{z}/{y}/{x}?format=image/jpeg`, EPSG:3857 zoom 0–20 and EPSG:4326; also `ortofoto_urbana`, `ORTOFOTOS_2019`, infrared and 1966 layers. A z17 tile at Minas: 23 kB JPEG, clear, no watermark.
- **Quick tests (2026-10-31)**: listings (545–588 sheets per national remesa), index DBF 6 597 rows; `/vsicurl/` `G24A1_MDT_Remesa_01.tif`: 2381 × 2160, open 2.3 s, 200 × 200 window 3.0 s; Minas 2025 MDS sheet: 0.1 m, LZW with overviews, 42 MB; licence read; WMTS tile.
- **Fit**: a new `IndexedTiles` subclass with a static shapefile index (sheet → remesa → URL), whole-file downloads; the half-odd origin means resampling unless the grid follows the file (`Source.grid_origin` per sheet). Effort M.
- **Highline**: Sierras de Minas/Lavalleja (Arequita, Villa Serrana), Quebrada de los Cuervos, Punta Ballena: low. Priority low (good data and licence, few cliffs, photogrammetric, no rural DSM).

## g13: northern South America (Colombia, Peru, Ecuador, Bolivia, Venezuela, Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana) (2026-10-31)

Scripts: `scripts/g13-sa-north/`, samples: `dl/g13-sa-north/`. Rows: [sources.json](sources.json) (8).
OpenTopography's catalogue (`ot_catalog.py`, all eight bounding boxes): besides the global sets, only ANADEM (a 30 m DTM for South America derived from Copernicus GLO-30, https://doi.org/10.5069/G9736P4G) and four small 2018–2020 fault surveys in southern Peru (Incapuquio, Purgatorio). No national lidar from any of these countries is hosted there or on the AWS Registry of Open Data.

### Colombia (IGAC): municipal DTM mosaics as public ArcGIS ImageServers, a patchwork of 1, 2, 5 and 10 m
- **Data**: IGAC's cartography projects (2013–2024, mostly 2021–2023, the cadastral "catastro multipropósito" programme) left photogrammetric DTMs per municipality: `MDT1_<DANE code of a town>_<date>` (1 m, the urban centre and villages, typically 0.1–4 km² each), `MDT2_<municipality>` (2 m, whole municipalities, e.g. Jericó 220 km², Jardín 203 km², El Peñol 120 km², 2023), `MDT5`/`MDT10` (5–10 m, whole municipalities, e.g. Suesca 174 km² at 10 m, Chaparral 1990 km²). DSMs (`MDS12_...`) exist only at 12 m (Atlántico 2014): useless. IGAC's portal lists **444 MDT and 63 MDS ImageServers** (`md/` folder) covering **284 municipalities** of ~1100, concentrated in Cundinamarca (91), Cesar (49), Sucre (35), Cauca (32), Bolívar (31); **Santander has only Vélez: nothing at the Chicamocha canyon**. A sample of 40 services (82 rasters): 1 m: 50 rasters / 155 km²; 2 m: 15 / 608 km²; 5–6 m: 3 / 1020 km²; 10 m: 8 / 3715 km². The `lowps` field of the catalog is not reliable (Suesca's 1 m raster says 10).
- **Highline places**: **Suesca**: only the town centre at 1 m (`MDT1_25772000_20220906`, 1.2 × 1.6 km; the rocks along the Bogotá river start at the town's edge, so only their northern end may be inside), the rest 10 m. **Guatapé / El Peñol**: El Peñol municipality has 2 m, but the Piedra del Peñol stands just inside Guatapé (05321), which has no service. Jericó, Jardín (Antioquia) 2 m: steep coffee-mountain terrain. Chicamocha, La Mojarra (Santander): none.
- **Access**: `https://mapas.igac.gov.co/image/rest/services/md/<name>/ImageServer` (ArcGIS Server 11.3, public, capabilities Image, Metadata, Pixels, Catalog, **Download**): `exportImage` (float32 TIFF, max 2071 × 2185 px for Suesca), `query` on the raster catalog (footprints, names with date), `download?rasterIds=` lists the source files (Azure blobs via `/vsiaz/`, e.g. Suesca's 1 m raster 7.5 MB, its 10 m raster 26 MB) and `file?id=` would serve them. The service list comes from the portal search `https://mapas.igac.gov.co/portal/sharing/rest/search?q=type:"Image Service"` (1065 items: 548 `orto`, 444 `mdt`, 63 `mds`, created 2021–2025). **The default mosaic puts the coarse municipal raster on top**: at Suesca a 1 m request returned 10 m blocks (90% equal neighbours); with `mosaicRule={"mosaicMethod":"esriMosaicLockRaster","lockRasterIds":[1]}` the true 1 m raster comes back. A driver must pick the finest raster per area from the catalog. **Services are cold**: the first request to a service takes ~30 s (instances started on demand), then 1–3 s. Colombia en Mapas (colombiaenmapas.gov.co), IGAC's download portal, needs a Google/Firebase sign-in for downloads and its API host `servicioscem.igac.gov.co` refused connections from here (also from WebFetch); the products there carry a licence field (CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, "Gubernamental", "Restringida", "Institucional").
- **Versions**: one raster per product and date; a municipality may have several dates (Tunja: MDT1 2022 and MDT10 2024; Balboa MDT10 2020 + MDT1 2022). Duplicate services exist (`mdt25386lamesa` and `mdt25386000lamesa`, `mdt25740sibate` and `mdt25740000sibate`).
- **Licence**: IGAC's open-data terms are Resolución 616 de 2020, which (per IGAC's summaries) adopts **CC BY 4.0** for its open data; "Estos conjuntos de datos son de acceso libre y gratuito". The ImageServer items themselves carry no licence text (`licenseInfo` empty on all 1065). The cadastral data are CC BY-SA 4.0. The resolution PDF is a scan (not read). Per product, Colombia en Mapas may mark data "Restringida"/"Gubernamental": unclear for these MDTs.
- **Orthophoto**: per-municipality orthophoto ImageServers (`orto/orto<code><name>`, e.g. Jericó 0.2 m, 4 bands U8, EPSG:9377, no tile cache): only `exportImage`, no WMTS mosaic; Colombia en Mapas itself shows Esri World Imagery as the base.
- **Quick tests (2026-10-31)**: portal search (1065 image services in 11 pages); catalog query of 40 services (results above, `igac_catalog.json`); exportImage 500 × 500 m: Suesca at 10 m (66 kB, 35 s cold) and at 1 m locked to raster 1 (1 MB, 2.6 s, float32, values on whole cm (100%), 4% equal neighbours, nodata absent: 0 outside the raster); Jericó at 2 m (263 kB, 2.7 s, 1830–1940 m, whole cm 100%, whole m 4%, 0.1% equal neighbours: real 2 m detail); El Peñol at 2 m over the reservoir (flat 1880–1907 m). CRS of every sampled service: **EPSG:9377 MAGNA-SIRGAS 2018 / Origen-Nacional** (the single national TM since 2020); vertical datum not stated (presumably orthometric, Colombian levelling / geoid GEOCOL). `download?rasterIds` answered with the files and sizes (no file fetched).
- **Fit**: new driver: **ArcGIS ImageServer exportImage** with an index of services (portal search, cached) and of their rasters (catalog footprints + resolution from the name), requesting the finest raster per block with `lockRasterIds` at its native pixel size and origin (grid in EPSG:9377). Alternatively the `download`/`file` operations give the source TIFFs (whole rasters of up to tens of MB). Effort L (two-level index, cold services, version choice). Value: limited to a few hundred towns and a few dozen 2 m municipalities, no DSM.
- **Open questions**: the licence of the MDT services (CC BY 4.0 under Res. 616/2020, or per product "Restringida"?); vertical datum; whether IGAC adds services (2025: one item); whether the `file` download is meant for public use.
- **Links**: https://mapas.igac.gov.co/portal/sharing/rest/search?q=type:%22Image%20Service%22&f=json, https://mapas.igac.gov.co/image/rest/services/md/mdt25772suesca/ImageServer, https://mapas.igac.gov.co/image/rest/services/md/mdt05368jerico/ImageServer, https://www.igac.gov.co/datos-abiertos/, https://www.igac.gov.co/datos-abiertos/datos-abiertos-geoespaciales, https://www.igac.gov.co/sites/default/files/transparencia/normograma/resolucion_616_de_2020.pdf, https://www.colombiaenmapas.gov.co/

### Peru (IGN Perú): nothing open beyond global models
- **Data**: IGN Perú makes the national 1:25 000 / 1:100 000 cartography (lately from PerúSAT-1 stereo imagery) and sells or gives products to public bodies; no national DTM/DSM finer than 30 m is published for download, and none was found in GEOIDEP (the national SDI) or in the national open-data portal. PerúSAT-1 imagery is distributed to state institutions (proprietary licence). Lidar exists only as small research surveys (the faults above) and local drone DEMs (CENEPRED's SIGRID library, e.g. Paucarpata 2018).
- **Access**: www.ign.gob.pe and geoservidor.minam.gob.pe did not answer from here (2026-10-31, connection timeouts); www.geoidep.gob.pe answers but its IGN page is gone (404).
- **Fit**: none (FABDEM/Copernicus). The Cordillera Blanca (Huaraz, Hatun Machay, Llanganuco) stays on FABDEM.
- **Links**: https://www.geoidep.gob.pe/, https://www.gob.pe/ign, https://sigrid.cenepred.gob.pe/sigridv3/documento/5341, https://doi.org/10.5069/G9JW8C3S

### Ecuador (MAG / SIGTIERRAS; IGM): a 3–5 m national DTM, but only 10 m online and CC BY-NC-SA
- **Data**: SIGTIERRAS (programme of the Ministry of Agriculture, MAG) flew the country in 2010–2014 and produced orthophotos (30 cm) and a photogrammetric **DTM of 3 m (Sierra), 4 m (Costa) and 5 m (Amazonía)**, vertical accuracy 1.5 m (Costa, Sierra) and 3 m (Amazonía); about 43 800 TIFF files on the IGM 1:5 000 sheets (~5.3 km² each), about 88% of the country, gaps filled from other DTMs. No DSM is published.
- **Access**: only the **10 m resampled version** is downloadable, through the "Gestor de Descarga de Información" (SINAT web, login required, area selected on a map and requested by form; some areas need a confidentiality agreement). "El MDT de full resolución puede solicitarse en oficinas." The download application (http://181.112.155.237/sinat_web_descarga/login, servicios.sigtierras.gob.ec) and www.sigtierras.gob.ec did not answer from here (connection timeouts, 2026-10-31); geoportal.agricultura.gob.ec answers on http only.
- **Licence**: contradictory: the CKAN dataset on datosabiertos.gob.ec says "Creative Commons Attribution Share-Alike (CC BY-SA)", its own metadata profile (XLSX, read) says "Licencia: CC BY-NC-SA, 4.0". Either way share-alike; NC would fit a non-commercial site but not redistribution under other terms.
- **Orthophoto**: SIGTIERRAS orthophotos (2010–2014) by the same download application; no public WMS/WMTS found.
- **Quick tests**: CKAN API `package_show` read; metadata XLSX read; download portal unreachable, so no file tested.
- **Fit**: none automatic. An own-hosting copy (GridTiles) would need the full-resolution files from the MAG office and a clear licence (share-alike, possibly NC). Highline: Cojitambo (Azuay), the Andes valleys (Baños, Cuenca); medium interest.
- **Open questions**: CC BY-SA or CC BY-NC-SA? Would MAG deliver the 3 m files for a whole province? Vertical datum of the files (probably ellipsoidal or EGM, unknown).

### Bolivia (IGM Bolivia, GeoBolivia): nothing open
No national DTM finer than SRTM is published; GeoBolivia (geo.gob.bo) did not answer from here (2026-10-31). Regional products (e.g. a Pléiades DEM for the Gobernación de Santa Cruz) are commissioned, not open. Highline interest (La Paz valleys, Torotoro) stays on FABDEM.

### Venezuela (IGVSB): nothing open
The Instituto Geográfico de Venezuela Simón Bolívar publishes basic cartography (a WFS listed by AmeriGEO) but no downloadable DEM; its own relief products derive from SRTM. The tepuis (Roraima, Auyán-tepui) are FABDEM only (and FABDEM/Copernicus are weak on vertical tepui walls).

### Guyana (GLSC): nothing open
Guyana Lands and Surveys Commission publishes no elevation data; no lidar found. Kaieteur gorge and the Pakaraima tepuis: FABDEM only.

### Suriname: nothing open
No national DEM; the former national GeoNetwork "Gonini" (gonini.org) now redirects to a gambling site (2026-10-31), so it is gone. FABDEM only (low relief, a few inselbergs such as Voltzberg).

### French Guiana (IGN, France): RGE ALTI 1 m in the Géoplateforme WMS-R, no LiDAR HD, no DSM
- **Data**: RGE ALTI 2.0 at **1 m and 5 m covers the whole of French Guiana** (D973): the download feed lists four 1 m quadrants (NE, NO, SE, SO) as TIFF and ASCII and one 5 m ASCII, edition 2023-01-01 (5 m: 2023-08-02); CRS RGFG95 / UTM 22N (EPSG:2972), heights NGG 1977 ("GUYA77"). Its sources are not stated per area (the source layer `RGEALTI-SRC_..._GUF` is only served at small scales and came back empty in a GeoTIFF); in the rainforest a 1 m DTM can only come from radar or sparse lidar, so the real detail inland is unknown. **LiDAR HD does not cover French Guiana** (IGN: "métropole + DROM (hors Guyane)"; the metadata WFS returns no tile in its bbox), so there is **no MNS (DSM)**. 20 cm orthophotos along the Oyapock (2023).
- **Access**: the same as metropolitan France (g3-fr-pt): WMS-R GetMap `image/geotiff` on `https://data.geopf.fr/wms-r/wms`, layer `RGEALTI-MNT_PYR-ZIP_GUF_UTM22RGFG95_WMS` (CRS EPSG:2972); or the archives of the download feed `https://data.geopf.fr/telechargement/resource/RGEALTI?zone=D973` (multi-part 7z of 4 GB parts: unusable on demand). The national layer `ELEVATION.ELEVATIONGRIDCOVERAGE.HIGHRES` (WGS84G) reaches west to -62.95 and south to -24, so it probably includes the DROM too (not tested).
- **Licence**: Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0, as for all IGN data since 2021 (commercial use and redistribution allowed with attribution).
- **Orthophoto**: the national WMTS `ORTHOIMAGERY.ORTHOPHOTOS` (PM grid, EPSG:3857) has French Guiana: a z19 tile over Cayenne 13 kB JPEG, sharp, no watermark; z20 404 (also `HR.`).
- **Quick tests (2026-10-31)**: WMS-R GetMap 500 × 500 m at 1 m and 5 m at Montagne du Mahury (Cayenne), the Nouragues inselberg and Saül: HTTP 200 `image/geotiff`, float32, 1 MB at 1 m in 0.6–1.6 s, nodata -99999, 100% valid, the file names EPSG:32622 (WGS 84 / UTM 22N, the same as RGFG95 in practice). Values: 18–46% on whole cm (interpolated); 0% equal neighbours (the service resamples bilinearly, so the native detail cannot be judged from it). LiDAR HD metadata WFS: 0 features near Cayenne.
- **Fit**: the planned France WMS-R driver (g3-fr-pt) with another layer and CRS (EPSG:2972, NGG77); gap filling against Copernicus needs the NGG77 offset checked. Only worth it as a cheap extra once France is done.
- **Highline**: low: granite inselbergs in rainforest (Nouragues, Mont Itoupé, Montagne Tortue), Îles du Salut sea cliffs are small; little climbing.
- **Open questions**: which source the 1 m DTM has inland (radar/correlation under canopy?); whether `HIGHRES` covers French Guiana.
